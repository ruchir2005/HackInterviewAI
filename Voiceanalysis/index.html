Scoring Formulas

How raw features are transformed into the 0–5 composite scores.

T

Tone Score

5 × (1 − |normalized_var − 0.5| × 2)

Rewards moderate pitch variance; penalizes both monotone and erratic delivery.

C

Confidence Score

5 − (jitter × 50) − (shimmer × 2)

Deducts from a perfect score for every bit of pitch/amplitude instability.

P

Pace Score

avg(pause_score, rate_score)

Averages sub-scores for pause ratio and speech rate; both need to be optimal.
